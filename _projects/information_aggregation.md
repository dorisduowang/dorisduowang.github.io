## Research question

Does team membership change how AI agents aggregate the same private information?

I designed 20 matched deliberations with 5 agents. Each of 5 private signal environments was replayed across 4 rosters. The task stayed fixed. So did the report assigned to every seat. Only the team changed.

## Measurement

The pipeline preserves every private report and public message before converting a run into 24 measures of deliberation and choice. I also reproduced 9 comparable measures for 70 human groups in the published condition without a facilitator.

The final city changed across rosters in 4 of 5 matched signal environments. Team composition therefore enters the mechanism. It is not background noise.
