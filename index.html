✦ About me

Hi, I’m Doris.

I’m an M.A. student in Computational Social Science (Economics) at the University of Chicago. I received my B.Sc. in Political Economy from King’s College London in 2025.

I’m drawn to questions about when cooperation breaks down, what people’s language reveals before it does, and how institutions can respond. I work with experiments, panel data, and agent simulations to study those questions at different scales.
