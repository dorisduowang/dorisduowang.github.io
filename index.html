Information economics · Feasibility study

Cheap talk and enforcement in repeated public goods games

What can cheap talk reveal about cooperation when sanctions depend on contribution history? In one 15-round feasibility run, I compare what four language-model agents say publicly with what they contribute in the next round. Next, I plan to vary how transparent sanction rules are and test when public communication helps groups sustain cooperation and when it helps players evade sanctions.
