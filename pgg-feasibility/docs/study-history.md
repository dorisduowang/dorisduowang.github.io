# Versioned study history

The latest announcement study contains 60 randomized blocks with 180 fresh openings. The earlier full-game pilot contains 6 blocks and 12 complete games. Their observations remain separate, and the historical record below preserves the development of the research question.

All payoff numbers are game tokens. A message count, model-return count or branch count is never presented as an independent sample size. The downloadable historical file contains whitelisted aggregates, not raw provider records or model reasoning.

## Corrections carried forward

- Withdraw the claim that aggregating private deductions necessarily adds threshold information under the known original scalar-threshold penalty family.
- A positive same-state ON-OFF difference does not imply a beneficial complete language policy.
- The fresh H/HL difference includes the announced rule and behavioral adaptation before sanctions can be active; it is not an isolated extra-ON effect.
- The old recipient-policy by inherited-reference interaction is zero. The proposed prospective control-by-update-policy interaction has not been measured.
- Reference-sensitive scores do not establish an empty trigger set, irreversible collapse, private overmanipulation or a social-externality theorem.
- Responses, messages, branches and repeated actions are not independent experimental groups. Recovered copies and shared prefixes are never new observations.
- Accurate numerical reports or coded dialogue summaries in a pilot do not prove universal sufficiency of every structured communication protocol.
- Mixed-model screening revealed positive ON effects at one history; it does not establish negative effects elsewhere or language information value.
- Free out-of-fold forecast analysis cannot supply unobserved ON counterfactual actions or convert prediction error into welfare gains.

## Latest announcement study

<a id="announcement-screen"></a>

Each of 60 randomized blocks contains 1 fresh H, SHADOW and LIVE opening.
SHADOW describes the same monitoring technology as LIVE while explicitly
excluding language scores from punishment and payoff rules. All 3 observed
rounds precede any possible active punishment.

Mean opening earnings are 325.28, 329.51 and 275.32 respectively. LIVE minus
SHADOW is −54.19 with pre-specified 98.75% interval [−70.37, −38.01]. SHADOW
minus H is +4.23 [−14.61, 23.07]. The pre-specified classification is closer
to H, without an equivalence claim.

The comparable LIVE minus H opening difference is −49.96, compared with −91.8
in the earlier full-game pilot. H full-cooperation counts are 9/60 and 4/6.
These new openings do not replace the older −367 complete-game estimate.

<a id="human-temporal-benchmark"></a>

### Human-data temporal prediction benchmark

**Status:** completed. **Version:** archival protocol; not pooled with current policy pilot.

Leave-one-session-out ridge comparison using original human-coded pregame requests. 110 archived human games, six sessions, 440 transitions.

The [Morgan, Mason and Shupp replication data](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/wz77fd99cz/1) use five-round games and a 25-point endowment. Messages are private pregame requests to a policymaker, not fresh public messages in every round. The homepage human panel and the fifteen-round agent trajectories therefore describe different protocols, not matched human-agent controls.

Request codes did not improve the equal-session error metric.

- The request-feature change in equal-session MAE was -0.0244997 under the source improvement convention; only two of six sessions improved.

Interpretation: This uses human-coded requests, not the DistilGPT2 trigger or a new agent experiment. Redistribution of the original human dataset depends on its source terms; this release includes derived aggregate facts only.

Provenance: `workspace:feasibility_midway/morgan_temporal/results.json`, `workspace:feasibility_midway/morgan_temporal/source_manifest.json`.

<a id="opacity-shelved"></a>

### Opaque-threshold proposal: withdrawn information claim

**Status:** shelved. **Version:** shelved after information-partition and transport audit.

Proposed hidden-rule chat/no-chat comparison, audited before native collection. Mathematical and software checks only; no native games.

The proposed private-fine aggregation advantage fails under the original known sanction family.

- Total own deduction is d_i=8q_i+sum_j q_j. For a common scalar threshold, monotonicity implies that equality of one player's deduction partitions candidate thresholds exactly as the full deduction vector does.
- The proposal also bundled threshold, formula, trigger and calculator disclosures.

Interpretation: This rules out a specific threshold-information argument, not every learning or coordination benefit of language. The archived execution code had replay/persistence defects; its software fixture is not usable research evidence.

Provenance: `workspace:pgg_opacity_check/review_20261126/CHECKS.json`, `workspace:pgg_opacity_check/review_20261126/REVIEW_中文.txt`.
